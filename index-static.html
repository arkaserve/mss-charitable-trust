Our Programs

Four Ways We Serve

Every program targets a specific gap — designed for lasting change, not temporary relief.

👧

Orphan Care

Safe shelter, daily nutrition, school enrollment, and psychological care for children without parental support.

  • Residential care & meals
  • School enrollment & tutoring
  • Counseling & skill-building
🙏

Widow Support

Economic independence and emotional resilience for widows through skills training and community support.

  • Vocational skill training
  • Monthly financial assistance
  • Legal awareness workshops
🏥

Free Health Camps

Qualified doctors and diagnostic services brought to rural communities that lack access to healthcare.

  • General health check-ups
  • Eye, dental & BP screening
  • Free medicine distribution
📚

Education Support

Scholarships, books, and mentorship to keep deserving students in school and on a path to opportunity.

  • Merit & need scholarships
  • Stationery & uniform kits
  • Career mentorship
